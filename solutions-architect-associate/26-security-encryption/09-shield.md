# AWS Shield

Managed distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) protection

## Shield Standard

- Automatically included at no additional charge
- Protects against common attacks such as SYN / UDP floods

## Shield Advanced

- Paid subscription for enhanced protection on supported resources
- Additional detection, visibility, and mitigation
- Shield Response Team access and DDoS cost protection, subject to eligibility
- Can integrate with WAF for application-layer protection
